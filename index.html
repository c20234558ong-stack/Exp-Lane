01

What the EXP Lane Is

Real Position on the Map

The map is a diagonal square with bases in opposite corners. The EXP lane is the outer path running along one edge of that diagonal, connecting the two bases without passing through the center jungle or river.

Why "EXP"

Minion waves here are farmed almost entirely solo, so all the experience from kills goes to one hero instead of being split. That hero levels up faster than anyone else on the team, hence the lane's name.

Who Goes There

One player, alone, for the entire early game. No support, no jungler lingering — just the laner, the minion wave, and whoever the enemy sends to match them.

Nearby Objective

The Turtle spawns in the jungle pocket just off this lane's edge. Whoever wins their 1v1 early usually gets to shove the wave and rotate in to secure or contest it.

Why Exp Lane

Latest Balance Change

MLBB rebalances roughly every two weeks — this reflects the most recent patch available and may shift by the time you read it.

Why Fighters & Tanks

Both classes are built to survive a 1v1 with no backup: high base durability, sustain, or damage that scales off levels and items gained through solo farm. They need to win lane on their own merit, not on rotations.

The Trade-Off

Because the exp laner is isolated, they're vulnerable to being ganked by the enemy jungler cutting across from the river. A good exp laner always tracks whether the enemy jungler is missing from the map.

03

How the Lane Plays Out

00:00 – 03:00

Laning Phase

Both exp laners farm the minion wave for gold and experience, trading hits or pokes when it's safe. Priority is last-hitting minions and not overextending near the enemy tower.

~03:00

First Turtle Spawn

Whoever is ahead in lane pushes the wave into the enemy tower, then cuts through the jungle pocket to help secure the Turtle — a small buff objective that grants team-wide gold and experience.

05:00 – 12:00

Split & Rotate

Once minion waves grow big enough to threaten towers alone, the exp laner alternates between farming, taking their own turret, and rotating through the river toward mid or Lord setups.

12:00+

Team Fight Phase

The exp laner, now a fully-itemized frontline or bruiser, joins the team for Lord contests near the enemy base and team fights, using the level and gold lead built up in lane to dive or tank for the squad.

04

Reading the 3D Map Above

Corner Bases

Blue base sits bottom-left, Red base top-right — mirror images of each other, each guarded by its own set of towers on every lane leading in.

Stacked Towers

Each turret is built from three layered slabs, so it actually rises off the board when the camera tilts, instead of sitting flat like a sticker. Gold-bordered towers guard Blue's approach; orange guards Red's.

Diagonal River

Cuts through the center of the map, separating the two jungles and giving junglers and roamers a fast path to gank either lane, including the exp laner.

Turtle & Lord

Turtle sits in the jungle pocket nearest the EXP lane's edge; Lord spawns later near the enemy's side of the river — both are why the exp laner's rotations matter so much.

Purple Monster Camps

Small jungle camps sit just off the lane on both sides. An exp laner who's shoved their wave can clear one for extra gold, or leave them for their jungler to save time on a clear.

Teal Minion Wave

The animated dots crawling up the lane represent the minion wave marching toward the enemy turret — the thing the exp laner spends the whole early game managing.
